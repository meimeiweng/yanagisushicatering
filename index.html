About Us

Meet Chef Yan

Chef Yan has over 20 years of experience as a sushi chef. His approach is simple but exacting: he believes the heart of sushi is "fresh fish, artful plating, and precise sauce pairing." Each bite is crafted to bring balance, color, and a smile.
